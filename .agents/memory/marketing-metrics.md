---
name: Marketing metric rules
description: Revenue availability and weighted aggregation choices for campaign analytics.
---

ROI requires actual revenue and positive spend. If any included row lacks revenue, mark the group's revenue and ROI unavailable rather than using a partial revenue total. Derive all efficiency metrics from summed inputs, not averages of row-level ratios.

**Why:** Conversions have no defined monetary value in the user's request. Inventing one or treating missing revenue as zero would misrepresent channel performance.

**How to apply:** Keep this rule consistent in overview totals, channel comparisons, campaign tables, and time buckets. Counts are counts; spend and revenue default to USD and imported files must use one currency.
